A Message from Pastor Steve

[OLD] Supernatural protection vs. Confidence in science & experts

Text of excerpt, if available

Our God is my refuge. God is my strength. God is my present help in time of trouble. God is our refuge. God holds us in the palms of his hands. He is our shelter in a time of storm. He is our protector. He is our sustainer. Nothing, nothing can touch his beloved child unless he allows it to, for his glory and for our good God is not only our refuge. He is our strength. Now is the time for each one of us to draw near to God, perhaps nearer than we've ever been. And to draw our strength from him. He is the source of our strength, our hope, our joy, our salvation. He will draw near to us as we draw near to him. And God is our very present, help in trouble. God will never leave or forsake his chosen children. All of us who have trusted in Christ for our salvation and in Ben, in dwelt, by his Holy spirit, God then is ever present with us and he will help us in our time of trouble.
