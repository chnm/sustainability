Palm Sunday Service 2020

Description

Good morning. Quinn. Chapel, family love you. So it's Sunday morning here, Palm Sunday. And it's time for us to have a word from the Lord. There is a word from the Lord. That word is a touch, but touch a simple phrase, a touch. You know the truth is we're living in an age right now where social distancing is a way of life and something. That's been so common to us as now, so far. And that is a touch. Can you remember how we used to greet one? Another brothers would talk it up. They give a good hand, slap reach over bump, shoulders, Pat, each other on the back, sisters, the walk up, touch hands, smile at each other, give each other a hug and everybody could touch somebody. But for now there is no touch. There's no tie folks right now. Some of us are really concerned about that because we're saying, will things ever be the same, but I want you to know some things won't be the same.
