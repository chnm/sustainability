[don't use] In-person communal gatherings (adapted)

Text of excerpt, if available

when you're coming back to mass and what a wonderful thing to be back in the churches when you're coming to mass on Sunday or during the week, we ask that you please come only through the center doors. The side doors will be used only to exit at the end of the mass. We've heard so much about purelling our hands. And when you come into church to the left of the center door, there is a Purell dispenser on the wall. If you put your hands on your neck, that will dispense a little bit of the hand sanitizer and you are more than welcome to sanitize your hands on your way to come to church.
