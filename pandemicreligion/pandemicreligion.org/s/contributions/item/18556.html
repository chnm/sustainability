Title

Woman sheltering under a scaffold at Riverside Church

Description

On my daily morning walk, I pass homeless people sheltering under Riverside Church's scaffolding. It is rare to see a homeless woman. Daily she washes her clothes and leaves it to dry on the railing. The Pandemic causes job loss; as a consequence, eviction from their apartments.

Date Created

August 8, 2020

Community

Riverside Church is a renowned NYC religious inter-denominational institution. Most churches closed their service social ministries due to COVID-19.

Denomination

Inter-denominational: American Baptist Church, USA, and United Church of Christ.

State

New York

Place

Upper West Side, Manhattan, NYC
