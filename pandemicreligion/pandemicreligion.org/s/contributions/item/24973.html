Title

Penn COVID-19 Community Archiving Project

Description

The Penn COVID-19 Community Archiving Project is headed by the University Archives and Records Center at the University of Pennsylvania. The prokect seeks to gather contributions from students, faculty, staff, and people who live near campus to document and preserve the impact of the pandemic on the Penn community. The project also seeks to document and collect experiences from diverse voices for future use by historians, doctors, scientists, public health experts, students, and scholars. Digital submissions can be uploaded using an online form, but physical objects are also accepted.
