And I can't emphasize that enough. Why else would the Lord call Saul of Tarsus if he didn't want some things to change and had to work for soul to do. And if we've been called out of darkness into his marvelous light, the Lord has a word for us to do. It's not all from the pulpit, but there's a ministry. There's a ministering that we can all do to one another. And to others, there's a light that needs to shine forth from us. There's a way that we're to be salt in this earth. There's an influence that we're to have a seasoning influence and a preserving influence. And the only preserving influence that we need in this nation is that of godliness. We need the Lord. We need the light of the Lord being shining forth from the people of this nation. We need that. And what have we just been through? I know that the government didn't stop Christianity, the government didn't undo anything God has done for us. But as far as public assembly, it's stopped.

That makes me tremble

That the Lord would allow that to happen to us. I know it was to get our attention, but if we, if we, if it doesn't get our attention, we're in sad shape going forward. We're in sad shape. If nothing changes,
