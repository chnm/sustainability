Title
In Solidarity Around the Shabbat Table
Description
Shabbat is about imagining a better world, and considering the actions we need to take to make that world a reality. Whether Friday night is a time to engage in powerful
conversation, or a time to take a break from the work of change-making, this is just the one of many ways we can amplify Black voices at our tables and prepare ourselves for
the work — and the world — to come.
