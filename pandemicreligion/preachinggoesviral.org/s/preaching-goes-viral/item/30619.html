We loved David's message. The invitation to trust. We love to be able to worship together in our home and our community group digitally over the last couple of weeks. And so thank you for all the work that you're doing. So after the Sunday gathering my CG hopped on a zoom call, we get our respective lunches were if you prayed and it has been so cool to see the Lord work in our group through prayer, I'm just repeating our own dependence upon him, but also really leaning on each other to lift each other up and intercede for one another. It's been so cool to see just that the process of prayer and what that looks like, bringing our group closer to that day.
