Silver lining: Blessed, grateful, or upbeat about the pandemic

Text of excerpt, if available

it's the painful  trials of life that will open people's  eyes to their blindness God works in the  midst of tragedy to draw people to  himself how many of the testimonies that  you've heard of people's baptisms have  begun in trials and suffering Jesus  shines his light in the darkness he  draws us near he reveals himself so when  things go wrong instead of asking who  should I blame a better question to ask  is what can this teach me about God how  could this help me to draw near to him  how could it be used to point others to  him and instead of assuming that God's  punishing you look for ways that God can  be calling you
