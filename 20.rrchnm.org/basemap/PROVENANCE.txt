protomaps-leaflet 5.1.0 (MIT)

protomaps-basemap-z0-5.pmtiles
  Extracted from the Protomaps daily planet build
  https://build.protomaps.com/20260811.pmtiles  (z0-15, ~120 GB)
  with:  pmtiles extract <planet> protomaps-basemap-z0-5.pmtiles --maxzoom=5

  Basemap layers derived from OpenStreetMap and Natural Earth.
  (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database License (ODbL).
  https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

  Zoom 0-5 covers every view the archive opens by default: the browse map
  (z4) and the per-item maps (z5 on 50 of 57, z4 on 4). Beyond z5 the vector
  renderer over-zooms the z5 geometry, which stays sharp but gains no detail.
