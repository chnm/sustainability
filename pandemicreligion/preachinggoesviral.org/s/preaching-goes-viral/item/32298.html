Text of excerpt, if available

So in the midst of a health crisis in this country, most likely the King is up there, isolated in his castle, and he's looking at the window and he's seeing these birds. He's seeing the protection of a mother bird, putting her wing over this helpless, weak, frail baby bird that can't take care of itself. And he says, I feel the same way about the Lord's protection and provision for me in the midst of this circumstance, he says, the Lord is my refuge. He is my fortress. I'm going to run into that fortress for protection and for safety in him. Well, I trust I'm not going to trust him then solely in the medical advances of men to STEM this pestilence, I'm going to trust in the Lord verse three, surely he shall deliver thee from the snare of the Fowler and from the noise pestilence, the notes boys, some pestilence.
