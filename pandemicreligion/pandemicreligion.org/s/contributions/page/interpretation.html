Interpretation

In addition to collecting items relating to religion and the COVID-19 pandemic, this project also seeks to understand what those items show us about how the pandemic is affecting religious groups across the United States. These are some interpretative essays that make use of the Pandemic Religion collection to tell parts of that story.

  • Bridget Bukovich, "Social Media Use During COVID-19."
  • Jess Pritchard-Ritter, "Congregation, Community, and COVID-19."
