Reboot: Forgiveness: A Visual Poem for a Visceral Time

This item originates from American Jewish Life.

Title
Reboot: Forgiveness: A Visual Poem for a Visceral Time
Description
"Forgiveness" is a visual poem that Reboot released with poet Aaron Samuels. The poem navigates the meaning of forgiveness and how it changes when looking at the context of the pandemic. The film is comprised of three parts which synthesis the learnings of the challenging journey of being Black and Jewish as well as the interpretations of violence against the Black community in modern day.
