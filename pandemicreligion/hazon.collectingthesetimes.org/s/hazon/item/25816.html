Summer 2021 Getaways: Isabella Freedman Jewish Retreat Center

Title
Summer 2021 Getaways: Isabella Freedman Jewish Retreat Center
Description
Unfortunately, we have had to cancel the planned Getaways program, scheduled for August 8th.

We hope that you will join us for our fall holiday retreats: Rosh Hashanah and Sukkahfest.

COVID has presented many challenges that ripple farther than the virus itself. As you may know, the food and hospitality industries have suffered, in particular, as employees have made permanent job changes in response to the virus. Because of these systemic industry changes, we have been unable to staff the retreat center for the getaways retreat in a way that would provide the right guest experience for our guests. In taking this week off, we are ensuring that our staff will be refreshed for our other upcoming retreats.
