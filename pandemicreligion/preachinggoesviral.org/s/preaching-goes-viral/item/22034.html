Title

Sermon Sunday May 3

Description

On the third Sunday in May, we would ask too, that our members, when we come back together, do not have handshaking or hugging before or after service. We will dispense what the handshake at the end of service. And we ask that our members have some distance between themselves and others. When we meet back on the third Sunday in May, that would normally be a lunch Sunday. However, under the circumstances that we have, there will be no lunch that Sunday and for the foreseeable future lunches will be canceled.
