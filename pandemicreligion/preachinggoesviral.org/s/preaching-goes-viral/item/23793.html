Title

Baptism - 25 July 2020 - 3 PM

Description

Therefore brothers and sisters, let us now prepare ourselves to participate in this celebration, listening to God's word, praying for this child in her family and renewing our commitment to the Lord and to his people. And so what name do you give your child? Kaylee Rose? What do you ask of God's church for Kaylee Rose in asking for baptism for your child? You are undertaking the responsibility of raising her in the faith so that keeping God's commandments, she may love the Lord and her neighbor as Christ just taught us. Do you understand this responsibility? Yes. Godparents, are you ready To help the parent of this child and her duty as a Christian mother, Kaylee Rose, the church of God receives you with great joy in her name. I sign you with the sign of the cross of Christ, our savior. Then after me, I invite your parents and godparents to do the same. And you can just take your thumb like I'm going to do, and you can bless her making the sign of the cross on her forehead. Great. And I invite you all to do the same
