Universality of the pandemic
Unity of religious community

Text of excerpt, if available

We believe we are all God’s equally loved children, regardless of religion, culture, or gender, and this includes our neighbors who have no religion. We also have seen time and again in our lives that the spiritual understanding of God as divine Love can bring actual physical healing to the human body, can redeem from sin, and bring harmony to every kind of problem.
