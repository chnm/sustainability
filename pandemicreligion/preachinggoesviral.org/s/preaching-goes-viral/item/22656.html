Title

A Time of Separation

Description

I think it was yesterday. And the woman was saying that after all this time off, we still haven't found 15 minutes that we could spend with the Lord. And I think that's the case in so many Christians lives that we have time. You can no longer use that as an excuse. Well, I just don't have time to read my Bible. We haven't had nothing but time. I don't have time to pray. How much time do you need? You got 24 hours a day. Now many of us are not going to work or where we went working from home.
