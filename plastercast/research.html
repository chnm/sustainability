Research

To all interested students: There are still identifications to be found, entries to be written, and features of these plaster casts yet to be discovered. You are welcome to participate in the future of George Mason’s Plaster Cast Project. Take a look at past projects and future ideas:

Cataloguing the casts:

In 2011, Stephanie LaSpada  designed a paper catalogue of the George Mason plaster cast collection. She received a scholarly award from OSCAR (Mason's Office of Student Scholarship, Creative Activities, & Research) for her work.

 Future Participants:

Students, GMU alumni, and individual scholars interested in using the collection for research should visit our Contact Us page.
