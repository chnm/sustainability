Baptism - 25 July 2020 - 3 PM

Text of excerpt, if available

Do your parents and godparents through the sacrament of baptism, the child you have presented as about to receive from the love of God, new life, by water and the Holy spirit. For your part, you must strive to bring her up in the faith so that this divine life may be preserved from the contagion of sin and may grow in her day by day. If your faith makes you ready to accept this responsibility, then mindful of your own baptism, renounce, sin, and profess faith in Christ Jesus, the faith of the church in which children are baptized. And in fact, I invite all of us to join together in these baptismal promises, we'll respond. I do first to the three renunciations of sin and then to the three statements of faith. And so do you renounce Satan? I do. And all his works I do. And all his empty show. I do. Do you believe in God, the father almighty creator of heaven and earth?
