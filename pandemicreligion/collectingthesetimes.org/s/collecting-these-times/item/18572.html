Title
Virtual confirmation held on August 7, 2020
Description
My son getting ready to participate in his virtual confirmation, August 7, 2020.
My son and his confirmation class of ten young adults from Temple Beth-El in Munster Indiana had their confirmation virtually on August 7, 2020. 50 or so participants, including family members and members of the congregation watched it live online. While there were minor technical glitches it still felt like a "communal" event and I am grateful we could all be together to celebrate these fine young people.
