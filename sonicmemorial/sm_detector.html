Sonic Browser

The Sonic Browser was the Sonic Memorial's online sound installation: a Flash 6 program in which each contributed recording was a line. Moving across the lines played the sounds, and choosing one showed its title, images and description, grouped by topics such as construction, identity, media and Windows on the World.

It no longer works. Browsers stopped running Flash in 2021, and the Sonic Browser loaded its recordings from a database on the original server that has not survived, so neither the program nor the collection it played can be shown here. The picture above is a screenshot from the project's 2003 press kit.

Many of the recordings were also published in the project's stories and lessons for educators, where they still play.
