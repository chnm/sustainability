Title

Fear Not Little Flock - March 15, 2020

Description

But I pray that the Lord would bless each one of his people today, as we think about and meditate upon how good that he's been to us to give us the privileges that we have in life, the privilege to assemble together as God's people. And in these times when we're not able to do that, it ought to renew in a suffrage, the desire to be more diligent in our meeting together and praying for one another and helping one another and loving one another as God’s dear children. Think about the fact that we have for years in my lifetime been, there's been an underlying concern that there might be some point at which the government would demand that God's people not assemble, not needing a public worship service if the wrong people were in charge of the government.
