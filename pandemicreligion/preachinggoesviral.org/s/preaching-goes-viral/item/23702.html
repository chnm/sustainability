Title

No Compromise- We May Burn But We Won't Bow

Description

Yeah, they can call it whatever they want to conspiracy theory. I have the facts. I know what's going on and I'm not going to back down. If you want me to be quiet, I'm going to get even noisier. If this stand was not going on right now, I can promise you that would have tried to shut the church down again. In this count in extra fact, they would love to do that, but we ain't shut down. Somebody said, well, it looks like you just want to cause trouble. Hey, I'm just doing what I do. We have church. I said, we have church. We preach the gospel. We lay hands on the sick. We cast out devils. We baptized in water. We have communion. We dedicate babies, Not from a distance with a squirt gun. And I'm not here to condemn anybody. If you're afraid for your health, I encourage you. Don't come. Really. Nobody's forcing you. Nobody forced you to go to Walmart. Amen. Nobody forced you to go to Costco. Nobody forced you to order Uber eats, but no one's forcing you to come to church. I can tell you that people year on this field or year, cause they want to be here today.
