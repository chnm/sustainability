Only God. Or... Not!; Prayer and faith as saving or duty

Text of excerpt, if available

And I am too. He says front and persuaded that neither death, nor life nor angels, nor principalities, nor powers, nor things present, nor things to come, nor height, nor depth, nor any other creature or coronavirus shall be able to separate us from the love of God, which is in Christ Jesus, our Lord, hallelujah. God is on his throne. Corona viruses, not more powerful than God. The natural flows that we have are not more powerful than God we made. We made falter and fall to the coronavirus or a sharp attack or a building falling down. Someone may shoot us and bug bogus in the middle of the night. Someone may do all that, but God is on his throne. And I tell you today that you ought to rejoice in him because you're secure in his hand. There's no way you could get out, but God still got you. Amen. Amen. Hallelujah. The doors of this church, church open for those resection of members. If you want to come and you feel like God's got you come here and rejoice with us as we try to walk in his way and in his path and be honoring and glorifying to his name. Amen.
