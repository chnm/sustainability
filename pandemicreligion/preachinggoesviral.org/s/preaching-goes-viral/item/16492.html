Transcript of Under the shadow of the almighty +D

Codes, tags, subjects, themes, topics

Medical Experts/Medicine; Only God. Or... Not!; Prayer and faith as saving or duty

Text of excerpt, if available

I want you to see how relevant God's word is for you today. And again, there's no new thing under the sun, the facts and the circumstances change and the generations change, but there is truly no new thing under the sun. So in the midst of a health crisis in this country, most likely the King is up there, isolated in his castle. And he's looking at the window and he's seeing these birds. He's seeing the protection of a mother bird putting her wing over this helpless, weak, frail baby bird that can't take care of itself. And he says, I feel the same way about the Lord's protection and provision for me in the midst of this circumstance, he says, the Lord is my refuge. He is my fortress. I'm going to run into that fortress for, and for safety in him. Well, I trust I'm not going to trust in solely in the medical advances of men to stand this pestilence.
