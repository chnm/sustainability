Title

Jen Miskov's Saturate OC experience

Description

Jen Miskov posted about her experience with Saturate OC on Instagram. The text below is the caption from her post:
"What a perfect way to celebrate the ending of my 10 Day online intensive School of Revival on Pioneering Revival by getting to go to the historic location where Lonnie Frisbee baptized hippies in the Jesus People Movement so many years ago. Worshiping Jesus together in the open air, seeing people get saved and baptized in water and also in the Holy Spirit, and getting to see God move in the midst of such challenging times all while at the beach enjoying God’s creation was so good for my soul. I met one lady who drove all the way from Arizona to be there. Her daughter was baptized and marked in God’s presence. Her friend and his son also came and was baptized in a powerful returning to Christ. Praise God for all the people who encountered and were awakened to His love. Thanks @jessi.green , Parker and @saturate_oc for saying Yes to leave everything behind in NY to obey what God called you to do here on the West Coast and tapping into some historic wells of revival here even when this looked very different from when God first called you. There will be ripple effects for eternity from your yes. Blessings and ? on the next leg of the journey, this is only just the beginning. #saturateoc #saturatemovement #iworeamask"
