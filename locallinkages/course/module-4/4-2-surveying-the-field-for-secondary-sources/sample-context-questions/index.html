Sample Context Questions

Project: What businesses did Chinese immigrants establish in Harlem in the 1920s & 1930s, and why?

Note: Your list of questions does not need to be this long

Local:
  • Who established the businesses operated by Chinese immigrants in Harlem?
  • When were they established? Where were they established?
  • What other businesses existed in Harlem?
  • Who else established businesses in Harlem?
  • When and where did other groups establish businesses in Harlem?
  • Did other groups establish the same businesses as Chinese immigrants?
  • Who worked in Harlem businesses?
  • Who lived in Harlem? Who patronized Harlem businesses?
  • What institutions and organizations existed in Harlem?
  • What were the legal restrictions on business in Harlem, for Chinese immigrants and for other groups?
  • How did local unions and community groups respond to Chinese businesses?

 

Regional / State
  • What businesses did Chinese immigrants establish elsewhere in NYC, and in other places in the US?
  • When were they established? Where were they established?
  • Were there legal restrictions on Chinese businesses in other places?
  • How did Harlem compare to other black neighborhoods in the northern United States?
  • How did the prosperity of the 1920s and the Depression of the 1930s impact Chinese businesses?
  • How did restrictions on immigration in the 1920s affect Chinese businesses?

 

Subject/Thematic
  1. Immigration
    1. How does Chinese immigration and American attitudes toward Chinese immigrants help explain the businesses they established?
    2. Were the businesses established by Chinese immigrants different before or after the 1920s and 1930s?
    3. How was the experience of Chinese immigrants different to that of other immigrant groups?
  1. Labor history
    1. Did the work involved in the businesses Chinese immigrants operated and who did that work change in the twentieth century?
  1. Asian Americans
    1. How did Chinese businesses contribute to the development of an Asian American identity?
