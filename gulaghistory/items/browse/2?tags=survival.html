Hospital Patients Recuperate Outside

Black and white group photograph of hospital patients convalescing outdoors at the White Sea Canal camp. They lay in wooden cots and are surrounded by hospital staff. Crutches are visible in the foreground, and one patient's leg is padded with evergreen boughs.

Doctor Examining a Patient

In the color sketch from her self-illustrated memoirs, Evfrosiniia Kersnovskaia is examined by Doctor Mardna, while Nurse Margarita stands at the end of the bed. In the accompanying text, Kersnovskaia describes how well they took care of her, and how she trusted them completely.
