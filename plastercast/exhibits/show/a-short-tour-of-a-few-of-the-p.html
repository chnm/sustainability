A Short Tour of a Few of the Plaster Casts at GMU

Take a virtual tour of George Mason casts.

Anna Jones has created a short tour for visitors to view a collection of the George Mason plaster casts. This tour includes a sample of casts from the following collections:

Art of Ancient Greece and Rome

Mediaval and Renaissance Art

Southeast Asian Art. 

This exhibit provides both a virtual experience and in-depth directions for those interested in viewing the casts online or at their designated location on campus.
