Module 5 Assignment

Drawing on the collection and research you have conducted and the skills developed in Modules 1-5, complete a digital local history exhibit in Omeka that answers your research question and presents and interprets primary sources that provide evidence for that answer.

Estimated time to complete this section: 7 hours

You should create:

  • At least 3-5 exhibit pages that each include primary source items and information from secondary sources, consider context, and present part of your answer to the research question
