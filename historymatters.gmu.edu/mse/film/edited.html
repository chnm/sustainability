Early filmmakers often created stories and illusions out of different, unrelated, film clips. In this exercise, you need to arrange these four early film clips to create a narrative. Choose an order for the clips that you think tells the best story and then select the Play Movie button. When you're finished, select the commentary button for more information on film editing.

Clips A through D (each is a few seconds long):


Clip A
Rough water rushes through a rocky river gorge past the base of a bridge.
Clip B
In a station lined with rows of columns, a train comes along the platform toward the camera; then a train with lit windows moves past in the dark.
Clip C
Seen from the front of a moving train, a small patch of light at the end of a dark tunnel grows until the train comes out into daylight and runs beside a river, under a bridge and along a hillside.
Clip D
Several people, among them a woman in a long light-colored dress, crowd through a doorway past a man in a dark uniform and cap.
