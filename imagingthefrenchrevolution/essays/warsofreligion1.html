[Depiction of massacre of Catholics by Protestants in Nimes, France, 1567]
Caption: (as provided in French): Voicy le Massacre faict a Nismes en Languedoc, auquel les Protestans se voulurent venger des catholiques qui parauant leur auoient faict beaucoup d'outrages. En ce Massacre furent tuez, plusieurs Consuls, Aduocats, Prestres & soldats, & une partie diceux, enuiron 30. ou 40. En nombre, furent iettez dans un puis. [Here is the massacre at Nimes in Languedoc where the Protestants took revenge on the Catholics who appeared  to have done many outrageous acts to the Protestants. In this massacre were killed several consuls, lawyers, priests, and soldiers and some others, around 30 or 40. A number were thrown in the well.] Anno MDLXVII. 1. D'Octo.
Source: Jacques Tortorel, Tableaux ou histoires diverses qui sont mémorables touchant les guerres, massacres et troubles advenus en France en ces dernieres années, par Jacques Tortorel et Jean Perissin. Suivi de gravures sur les guerres des Pays-Bas. (N.p., ca 1581) 139 col.
Medium: Plates, hand-colored. Lists of plates in French; captions in German, a few in German and French.
