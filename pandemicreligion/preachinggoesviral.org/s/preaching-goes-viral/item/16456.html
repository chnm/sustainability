Transcript of God and Pestilence +G

Codes, tags, subjects, themes, topics

Suffering; Blame who or what for the virus?; Only God. Or... Not!; Punishment -- pandemic as punishment or curse.

Text of excerpt, if available

I don't know if it's from God or not sounds to me like he can't use it. I don't know if it's, Oh, you know, human error, negligence, whether it's just a result of a sin, cursed and fallen. But God uses pestilence to judge his people. I've proven that. And if you want more proof, just read the old test. I mean, all of it's all in there, but God is long suffering and always grants us space to repent. You remember? He said, he's very long suffering. You remember that one back in numbers or maybe it was in a little bit. He says, I'll do this. And then he said, but if you don't do this and seven more times, seven more times, seven more times. And yet for all this, I'm not going to hoard them.

God's long stuff. S
