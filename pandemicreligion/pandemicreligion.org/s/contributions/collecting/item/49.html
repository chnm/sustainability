Collected Item: “Pandemic in the Midst of Junior Year”

Title

Pandemic in the Midst of Junior Year

Narrative or description

I am a 21-year-old attending Missouri State University and currently a junior. I attend a Christian church every Sunday. Although due to the global pandemic, my church has had to close it's doors. I now watch videos posted weekly on my church's website of our pastors preaching.
I believe that this pandemic would be impossible to survive without technology at this time. I also meet with my small group through my church weekly as well via Zoom calls. This has been a major transition and not one that anyone is overly thrilled about, but I am thankful that I can still have a sense of community even from afar.
