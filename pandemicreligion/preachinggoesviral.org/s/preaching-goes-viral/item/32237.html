If you're troubled, whatever the situation may be, whatever it is that causes you worry or anxiety, pour out your heart before him go to the Lord and say, well, he knows all about it. Yes, but he invites us and encourages us to come and pour out our hearts and just tell him exactly how you feel. What's troubling you. God is a refuge for us. He is willing to help.
