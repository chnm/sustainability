And I just thank God for the team that he has assembled and placed around me. And so let's talk about why we feel like this is a safe place for us to regather. First of all, we have hired a professional cleaning company who will sanitize and disinfect our facility prior to our regathering. This company really is, is just taking care of facilities in many different States. And so they're going to come and they are going to help us. And the disinfectant that they put on hard surfaces touch surfaces have a 30 day protection applied to that. So we feel good and confident about that. Also we ourselves are going to disinfect and we are going to sanitize before every service in between every service and also after every service. So we felt like the cleaning aspect of things we have that covered. We have also as much as possible created a hands-free environment.
