All films, including films that tell fictional stories, contain ideas about politics, society, and morality. In order to understand the ideologies embedded in films, it is important to know something about who made films and how the public reacted to them. View these two clips from The Birth of a Nation (1915), directed by D. W. Griffith, and Within Our Gates (1919), directed by Oscar Micheaux. What can they tell you about the filmmakers' intentions, and how do the directors use film techniques to convey their messages? Read the Commentary for more information about the films.

The Birth of a Nation
Within Our Gates
Description of this clip

In an office with a desk and a lit candle, a man in a dark suit (the character Silas Lynch, played by a white actor in blackface) speaks with a young woman in a pale dress with a cross at her collar. She draws back and points him away. An intertitle reads “Lynch's reply to her threat of a horsewhipping for his insolence,” followed by a shot of a crowd filling a town street. A second intertitle reads “See! My people fill the streets. With them I will build a Black Empire and you as a Queen shall sit by my side.” Lynch advances on the frightened woman and seizes her hand. The clip then cuts to men in white Ku Klux Klan robes and hoods riding horses through woods and galloping along a road, ending on a red-tinted shot.

  
Description of this clip

An intertitle reads “Sylvia, hidden the night with relatives who had promised to help her parents, remains ignorant of their fate.” Short shots follow: a woman at a table, a man pulling open a shuttered door, a frightened woman, and an older white man in a white shirt and tie at a doorway. A second intertitle reads “Not satisfied with the poor victims incinerated in the bonfire, Gridlestone has come searching for Sylvia.” In a dining room the older man grabs a young Black woman, Sylvia, and she struggles to get away from him around the table and across the room; the struggle is intercut with shots of a raging fire. A brief final shot shows a woman and a man seated together in another room.
