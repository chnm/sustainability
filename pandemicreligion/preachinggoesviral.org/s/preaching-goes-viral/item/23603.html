Behind Closed Doors - April 12, 2020

Description

We want to remind you that the Lord is right there with you. No matter what you're going through, he is there. His presence is there, right in your living room, right in your, your bedroom, hallelujah. Right there in the car, wherever you are in the kitchen, he is there. And the song says he is for you. Not against you. Even in this season that we're going through. He is faithful.
