And they would have to take it to the mikvah. Yeah, they could probably catch her there, Kelim, but apparently that was too complicated for them or for their Kelim. So other avenues had to be addressed. Of course, the best thing to do is to take the Kelim into the ocean or the sea, but that doesn't work. If you don't live near one, rivers are problematic as we've discussed already. And you know, you could consider using plastic or aluminum Kelim only, but as much as possible, but that's not a practical solution for cooking, especially.
