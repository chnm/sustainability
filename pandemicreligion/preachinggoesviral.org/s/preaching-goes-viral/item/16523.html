Text of excerpt, if available

To add to this dark picture, we have seen the video played and replayed of a Minneapolis policeman killing a black man.It was a horrible sight and brings deep grief to think that such a thing would happen in America.

But then we have seen depravity on display as rioters have destroyed businesses, shot at policemen and others, and one shop owner beaten almost to death trying to defend his property.

So these are trying, difficult times. The question for us to consider is: how are we responding to the troubles which are touching our lives? Through trouble God teaches us; He teaches us the truths of His word and how to apply them. So we want to think biblically about our troubles.
