Title

Cousins monthly get together turned into Zoom happy hour in Atlanta!

Description

The Bregman clan getting together once covid hit to continue our monthly celebrations with one another!
What I have found is that being with each other is precious! We do not often take time from your busy lives to connect but now we have all learned how important it is. Reach Out to practice safe distancing but not social isolation!
