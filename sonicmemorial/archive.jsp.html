This is a static copy of the final website maintained by the Roy Rosenzweig Center for History and New Media.
Hundreds of people have contributed personal and historic audio artifacts to this archive. You also will find interviews and oral histories from the last 70 years. You can search them by theme, name, noun, or date.

Visit the Sonic Browser to explore these sounds in an immersive soundscape. Do you have a sound or story to contribute?

Archive

The archive let visitors search and listen to the more than 1,000 stories, sounds and images contributed to the Sonic Memorial Project. It ran on a database on the original server, which has not survived, so the archive cannot be searched or played here.

Many of the recordings were published in the project's stories and lessons for educators, where they still play.
