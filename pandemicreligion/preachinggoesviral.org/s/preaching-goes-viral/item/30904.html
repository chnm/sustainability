Do you think Paul slowed down? Whoop. I'm a suffering. I'm going to take a few weeks off. Lord, I know I'm supposed to go to Rome next week, Lord. I know I'm supposed to preach to this group and that group next week, can't do it. No. Paul continued to work. Paul continued to serve. I'm going to tell you one of the things, frankly, that has upset me about the Corona viruses. I have seen far too many, just quit.
